4 · Validation

Expanding walk-forward: train on everything before the year, test on that year (2020→2026). The model has never seen its test days. Ensemble stack fits on train-side probabilities only.

6 · Honesty

In-sample fit (what papers usually report), a fair 80/20 time split, and walk-forward OOS — all shown. No cherry-picking, no lookahead, no survivorship. Past performance ≠ future results.
