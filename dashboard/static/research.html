THE HONEST NUMBERS

A rigorous, leak-free follow-up to the IJFMR paper claiming 87.4–90.1% accuracy on NIFTY options ML. Three evaluation protocols, Black-Scholes backtests, and one strategy with a real out-of-sample edge.

FINDING 1 The paper's 89–90% is mostly its label design — we measured it

The paper labels a day BUY only if the next day moves > +1%; every other day is SELL. On NIFTY, SELL is ~87% of all days. We trained the same kind of XGBoost on that exact label with a clean 80/20 time split:

FINDING 3 Walk-forward out-of-sample: daily direction is at chance

Every directional model sits at ~chance on strictly out-of-sample yearly blocks (OOS 2020-01-01 → 2026-04-10): 3-class accuracy 40–48%, up/down 50–54%, AUC 0.49–0.52. A linear probe on the raw 64px charts scores 100% in-sample and 36% out-of-sample (below the 44% majority baseline) — the exact overfitting fingerprint that produces the paper's table. Daily NIFTY direction is essentially unlearnable from these features.

The directional OOS backtests still print big returns (ViT +1,115%, XGBoost +818%, Ensemble +379%) because NIFTY rose ~97% over that window and a long-option book leverages a bull trend. Win rates 49–52% (coin flip), Sharpes 0.6–1.0 vs Buy & Hold 0.70 — that P&L is market beta, not forecasting alpha.

FINDING 4 The vol-expansion straddle alpha: the real edge

Direction is coin-flip; volatility expansion is not. A walk-forward XGBoost model of "will the next 5-day move exceed the IV-implied range?" reaches — OOS AUC (53.9% accuracy vs 46.2% base rate), year by year AUC 0.48–0.67 across 2020–2026. Traded as a fixed ₹10,000 long ATM straddle on vol-expansion days (5-day hold, Black-Scholes entry, intrinsic exit):
